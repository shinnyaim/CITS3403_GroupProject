How To Play

Goal

Survive 14 days of university group project chaos. Make decisions, manage your teammates, and submit your project before the deadline. Every choice you make affects your Progress and Morale — balance both to get the best grade possible.

The Basics

Four simple steps to get started

1.Account

Create an account by signing up, or log in, to save your progress. Your runs, grades, and history are tied to your profile.

2.Your Team

Before you begin give your team a name. At the start of each run, 3 teammates are and assigned to your group.

3.Event Cards

Each day draws a new event card. Read the situation and pick one of the three choices presented to you. Each choice will have an effect on your progress and morale

4.Get Graded

After 14 days (or earlier if you choose), your final grade is determined by your progress, morale and how long it takes you to submit.

Progress

Your Teammates

3 are randomly assigned at the start of every run

The Overachiever

Does everything their way — whether you asked or not. Great for progress, rough on morale.

The Ghost

Reads messages but never replies. Disappears when work is due. Where did they go?

The Slacker

Full of ideas, short on output. "I'll do it tonight" — they won't do it tonight.

The Critic

Tears apart every draft. Sometimes useful. Mostly exhausting. Your work is never good enough.

Event Cards

Each day, a new card presents a unique challenge. Choose wisely!

The Disappearing Act

"Josh has not responded to any messages for 4 days. His section was due yesterday. The group chat shows he has read every single message."

Pick one of three options:

Option A

Cover for Josh and split his work among the group. Progress +5, Morale -10.

Option B

Send a firm message to the whole group calling it out publicly. Morale -5, but Josh finally responds.

Option C

Do nothing and hope for the best. No immediate change, but the deadline inches closer.

Game Over Conditions

The run ends when one of these happens

14 Days Are Up

The deadline has arrived! Your final grade is calculated based on whatever progress and morale you finished with.

Morale Hits Zero

Your team has completely fallen apart. The project is abandoned and you receive a failing grade.

Early Submission

Feeling confident? You can choose to submit before Day 14. Risk vs reward — is your progress high enough?
